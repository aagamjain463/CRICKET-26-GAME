### What the sequence is now

Run-up cadence is derived from the stride's own geometry
(`C26Motion::StrideSpan/StrideSupport/StrideCadence`), so the support foot stands
still in the world. The pace bowler's span SATURATES with speed instead of being
pinned to a 20 cm floor, and his duty factor falls .60 -> .28, so turnover builds
~1.2 -> 3.6 steps/s and holds while the stride lengthens — before it ran ~7.7
steps/s and SLOWED as he accelerated. The bowler's speed filter is 26 (was 9):
his approach is already a smooth authored curve, so the filter could only lag,
and a lagging speed = stride shorter than ground covered = the skate.

Delivery: wider entry blends (Blend .13 -> .18, new TorsoBlend .22), an authored
per-phase ankle-pitch table, pelvic/torso ease-in, and the bowling hand uncurling
across `C26Field::ReleasePoseTime` (release stays the game mode's call).

Follow-through: exit speed restated from the run-up's own numbers
(1705 / RunUpDuration) and decaying quadratically over 1.23 s, so he leaves the
crease at the speed he arrived at instead of checking at release. Window
1.25 -> 1.85 s. Clip warp Length 1.34 -> 2.20.
