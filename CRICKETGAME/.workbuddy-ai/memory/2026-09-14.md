## The two root causes

1. **Single-period idle + non-monotone trigger.** The striker used the same
   `C26Motion::Rest()` single-sine-per-channel idle as every fielder, so he
   looped visibly; and the trigger ran through `sin(Load*PI)`, which peaks at
   half load and returns to zero on the delivery stride — the batter loaded his
   backlift while the bowler ran in and put the bat back down on the ball.
   Fixed with `C26Motion::BatterIdle` (four incommensurate periods + a gated bat
   tap) and `C26Motion::BatterTrigger` (`Press` monotone in load; separate
   `Step`/`Lift` curves so the relocating foot takes a step instead of scraping).

2. **A silent IK clamp that no gate could see.** `two_bone()` clamps an
   over-long target and returns a `reachable` flag that the authoring pass threw
   away. The front leg was asked for up to 9.0 cm more than it has, so the foot
   never reached the ground — the front ankle sat 7.1 cm high at contact and the
   corrector preserved that as a whole-body lift. **Fixed by driving the hips
   FORWARD over the front foot (`hips_y` 0.00 → 0.27), not by lowering them**;
   lowering raises the hip→foot span and makes it worse. Front knee went from
   locked-straight (100.5% extension) to 162→126→162°.

## Reusable lesson (the important one)

`worst_skate()`-style checks compare only frames a foot is **already** down on,
so a foot that never arrives at the ground is invisible to them. Any "this limb
is planted/attached" gate needs a companion gate that measures the limb's
**distance from its own target**, not just frame-to-frame stability.

Also: when a limb is short of its target, check the CHAIN LENGTH first. The
clamp ceiling is `(L1+L2)*0.999` — at f23 the realized ankle sat exactly on the
hip→target ray at 76.28 cm while the chain is 76.36 cm, which identified a clamp
rather than a solve bug.

## The imported clip is verified — and how
`Tools/VerifyImportedClip.py` (added this session) loads the imported
`UAnimSequence` and re-asserts the drive's geometry in engine cm. **10/10 PASS**,
agreeing with the offline corrector to 0.1 cm: stance ankles 24.7/24.7, hips
184.5, hand span 17.9, planted-foot worst lift 1.1 cm, weight 37.2 cm forward,
front-foot stride 35.8 cm, contact hands 49.0 cm in front of the hips, head
107.1 cm above the hips, and **chest 36.5 deg off square** — which matches the
corrector's own `chest closed +36.5 deg off square` exactly. The import is
faithful.

**Reference-direction trap, and the near-miss it caused.** For a batsman the
back-foot-to-front-foot line is NOT "down the pitch" — his feet are largely side
by side across the crease, so that line is mostly LATERAL. Using it as the
forward reference made the shoulder line look anti-parallel (dot −0.998) and I
briefly suspected a whole-body 180° twist. It is not one: the chest NORMAL is
36.5° off the direction of travel, which is exactly a textbook slightly-closed
stance, and the shoulder line is 90 + 36.5 = 126.5° from it, which is what was
measured. **Use the direction the body TRAVELS (hips f0 → contact) as the
bowler reference; it cannot be fooled.** Both checks are now permanent gates,
and the chest one doubles as the 180°-yaw guard the pipeline has needed since
`repair_facing` (a twist sends the chest normal to ~143° and fails).

**Composition gotcha, worth remembering:** with `u.Transform()` accumulation in
the Python API, iterating `find_bone_path_to_root()` in its **natural order (bone
first, root last)** is what reproduces the offline numbers; reversing it to
root-first does not. That is a fact about *Python's* multiplication semantics and
is independent of the gate bug above — do not "fix" the order. (Note the C++
convention is the opposite: `Acc = Acc * Local(child)` walking root-first is
correct there, which is what the gate already does.)

## Environment notes worth keeping
- **`AddInfo()` in an automation test is NOT printed** (the info array is
  swallowed by `-testexit`). Use `UE_LOG(LogTemp, Warning, TEXT("DIAG ..."))` to
  get values out of a test into `-stdout`. This is the cheap way to find out what
  a geometry gate is *actually* measuring instead of arguing about it.
- **When a rig measurement looks insane, print a SINGLE bone with no
  composition.** One line — `GetBoneTransform` for `Hips` alone — separated
  "composition order is wrong" from "the whole measurement is in the wrong
  frame", which two rounds of reasoning had not. The giveaway was that the
  composed and uncomposed values were *identical*.
- **Refute a fix hypothesis by rebuilding, not by argument.** Two plausible
  causes for `AuthoredClips` were each tested with one build+run cycle and both
  died on contact with data. Cheap, and it is what kept a wrong fix out of the
  commit.
- `UnrealEditor-Cmd` needs the **absolute** `.uproject` path; a relative one
  silently resolves against the engine binary dir and fails to open the project.
- UE tooling needs the sandbox bypassed (writes to `Content/`, `Artifacts/`, logs).
- `grep -a <literal>` intermittently returns nothing in this shell; `grep -aE`
  works. Prefer the Grep tool.
- PIL only at `/Library/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/3.14/bin/python3`.
- Other worktrees run UE concurrently, so harnesses can be slow.
